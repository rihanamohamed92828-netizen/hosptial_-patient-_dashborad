Report notes

England-level monthly activity and performance. Latest reporting period shown at right.

Measure definitions

Four-hour performance is the share of attendances admitted, transferred or discharged within four hours. Long waits are patients spending more than 12 hours from decision to admit to admission.

Comparability

Earlier activity figures are estimates before June 2015. Interpret four-hour comparisons cautiously during the Clinical Review of Standards field-test period, May 2019–May 2023.

Source

NHS England, A&E Attendances and Emergency Admissions monthly time series. Aggregate statistics, not individual hospital or patient records.
